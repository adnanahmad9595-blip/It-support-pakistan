How to use this tool

  1. Select your Excel or CSV file.
  2. Choose the conversion that appears.
  3. Download the converted file.

Tips

  • CSV stores plain values only, so formatting, colours and formulas are not kept.
  • Check the result in a text editor or spreadsheet before importing it elsewhere.
  • Large spreadsheets depend on your device memory.

Questions

Do formulas survive in CSV?

No. CSV stores the displayed values, not the formulas.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.
