How to use this tool

  1. Select your Excel or CSV file.
  2. Choose the conversion that appears.
  3. Download the converted file.

Tips

  • This makes a simple table. Complex layouts, charts and exact Excel formatting may not carry over.
  • For wide sheets, check that all columns are readable in the PDF.
  • Keep the original Excel file for editing.

Questions

Will the PDF look exactly like my Excel sheet?

No. It creates a clean table, not a pixel copy of the sheet.

Is my file uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.
