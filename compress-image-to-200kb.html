How to use this tool

  1. Select your image (or several at once), or drop it on the box.
  2. Choose JPG or WebP, then the size or percentage.
  3. Press Compress Image, then Download.

Tips

  • At 200 KB most photos keep good detail on screen.
  • Scanned documents stay readable, but check small text before you upload.
  • If a portal rejects the file, check whether it also needs exact pixel dimensions and use Resize.

Compress many photos, or take a new one

Select up to 30 images at once and compress them all to the same size, then download them one by one or as a ZIP. See Compress Multiple Images. No photo ready? Press Take photos to open the camera inside this page, shoot one or many photos and compress them straight away. The photos are not saved to your phone gallery. Read more about compressing a photo from the camera.

Questions

Can I compress a PNG to 200 KB?

Yes. Choose JPG as the output for the smallest, most widely accepted file.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens inside your browser.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.

Can I process several images at once?

Yes. Select up to 30 images together, process them in one go, then download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.

Can I compress a photo straight from my camera without saving it to my phone?

Yes. Press Take photos, shoot your picture inside the page, then compress it. The photo is not saved to your gallery. A file is saved only when you press Download. See Compress Photo from Camera.
