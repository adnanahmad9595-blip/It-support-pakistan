How to use this tool

  1. Select your PDF.
  2. Choose Light or Strong mode.
  3. Press Compress PDF, then download.

Tips

  • Light mode keeps text selectable and typically saves 10–25%.
  • Strong mode turns pages into images, which can cut scans and photo PDFs a lot. Text can no longer be selected.
  • If the result is still too big, split the PDF and send it in parts.

Questions

Which mode should I pick?

Use Light for text documents and Strong for scanned or photo-heavy PDFs where size matters most.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no limit on our side, but very large files depend on your phone or computer memory.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.

Can I make a PDF from photos or from my camera?

Yes. Open Image to PDF, select images or take photos with the camera, then save one PDF, one long page, or a separate PDF for each photo. You can also scan pages with Camera to PDF.
