Edit PDF pages online, free

Most PDF jobs are about pages: one is sideways, one is blank, one is in the wrong place, or you need to add a photo. This tool lets you fix all of that in your browser, with no login. Your PDF is not uploaded.

How to edit a PDF

  1. Open the Edit PDF tab and press Select PDF.
  2. Your pages appear as small pictures. Use the buttons under each page.
  3. Press Save PDF and download the new file.

What you can do

  • Rotate pages left or right.
  • Delete pages you do not need.
  • Reorder pages with the arrow buttons.
  • Add images from your gallery, or scan a page with your camera. iPhone HEIC photos work too.
  • Edit a page like a scan: tap the pencil to crop the corners, rotate and apply a filter.

Good to know

  • Pages you only rotate, delete or reorder keep their original content.
  • A page you edit like a scan becomes a picture, so its text can no longer be selected.
  • This is a page editor, not a text editor. It does not change the words inside a page.
  • Password-protected PDFs cannot be opened, and PDFs are limited to 100 pages.

Questions

How do I edit a PDF online for free?

Select your PDF, then rotate, delete or reorder pages, add images or scanned pages, and press Save PDF.

Can I rotate a page?

Yes. Use the rotate buttons under the page. Rotated pages stay as normal PDF pages.

Can I delete pages?

Yes. Press the cross under a page to remove it, then save.

Can I change the text inside the PDF?

No. This tool works with pages. If you edit a page like a scan, it becomes a picture, so its text is no longer selectable.

Can I add an image or a new page?

Yes. Add images from your gallery, or scan a page with your camera, and it is added as a new page.

Can I open a password-protected PDF?

No. Protected PDFs cannot be opened in this tool.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is edited in your browser, on your own device.

Is there a page limit?

You can edit PDFs of up to 100 pages here.
