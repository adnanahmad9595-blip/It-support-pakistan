How to use this tool

  1. Select your image (or several at once), or drop it on the box.
  2. Choose JPG or WebP, then the size or percentage.
  3. Press Compress Image, then Download.

Tips

  • Choose JPG as the output if the form asks for JPG. WebP is smaller but not every form accepts it.
  • No file yet? Press Take photos to shoot with the in-page camera, then compress right away. Nothing is saved to your gallery.
  • Signatures and simple passport photos stay sharp at 50 KB. Large, detailed photos may look a little softer.
  • Resize the photo first with the Resize tool. Smaller dimensions let the compressor keep more quality.

Questions

Will my photo look blurry at 50 KB?

A simple photo or signature stays clear. A large detailed photo is also reduced in dimensions, so it can look softer.

Is the result exactly 50 KB?

It is at or very close to 50 KB. If a portal is strict, choose a slightly lower size such as 45 KB.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.

Can I process several images at once?

Yes. Select up to 30 images together, process them in one go, then download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.

Can I compress a photo straight from my camera without saving it to my phone?

Yes. Press Take photos, shoot your picture inside the page, then compress it. The photo is not saved to your gallery. A file is saved only when you press Download. See Compress Photo from Camera.
