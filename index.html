Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only provide remote support, or do you visit on-site too?

Most issues are resolved quickly over a remote session. For hardware installs or larger jobs, an on-site visit can also be arranged.

We're a small office — is this only for big companies?

No. Solutions are scaled to your size and budget — a small office gets a right-sized setup, not an unnecessarily expensive one.

Which software should we actually use for security and backups?

It depends on your team size, budget and what data you handle. We assess your setup first and recommend specific, cost-effective software rather than a generic list.

How long does it take to fix an issue?

Small issues are usually resolved in the same session. Larger or network-wide issues depend on the specifics, and we give you a time estimate upfront.

How is pricing decided?

Based on the nature and time of the job — message us on WhatsApp with the details for a quick estimate.
