How to use this tool

  1. Select your image (or several at once).
  2. Enter the width and height, or tap a preset.
  3. Press Resize Image, then Download.

Tips

  • Requirements differ by country and form, so check the exact pixels and file size asked for.
  • Untick Keep proportions for the exact size. For a natural look, start with a photo of similar shape (about 7:9).
  • After resizing, use the Compress tab if the form also sets a KB limit.

Resize many photos, or take a new one

Select up to 30 images at once and resize them all to the same dimensions, then download them separately or as a ZIP. No photo ready? Press Take photos to shoot one with the in-page camera and resize it straight away.

Questions

Is 413×531 the right size for my passport?

It matches 35×45 mm at 300 DPI. Always follow the dimensions your authority publishes.

Does resizing reduce quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Making it larger cannot add detail.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.

Can I process several images at once?

Yes. Select up to 30 images together, process them in one go, then download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.
