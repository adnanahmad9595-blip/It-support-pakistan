How to use this tool

  1. Select your PDF.
  2. Type the pages you want, such as 1-3, 5.
  3. Press Split / Extract Pages, then download.

Tips

  • Use commas for separate pages and a dash for ranges, for example 2,5,8-10.
  • Extract the pages you need before sharing, so you do not send the whole document.
  • Page numbers start at 1 for the first page of the file.

Questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Type them separated by commas.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.

Can I make a PDF from photos or from my camera?

Yes. Open Image to PDF, select images or take photos with the camera, then save one PDF, one long page, or a separate PDF for each photo. You can also scan pages with Camera to PDF.
