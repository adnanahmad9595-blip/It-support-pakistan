How to use this tool

  1. Select your image (or several at once).
  2. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP.
  3. Press Convert Image, then Download.

Tips

  • PNG does not improve a JPG. It keeps the pixels as they are, so the file usually gets larger.
  • Choose PNG for logos, screenshots and graphics with sharp edges.
  • If size matters more than editing, keep JPG or choose WebP.

Convert many images, or take a new one

Select up to 30 images at once and convert them all to the same format, then download them separately or as a ZIP. Press Take photos if you want to shoot new pictures first. The photos are not saved to your gallery.

Questions

Why is the PNG bigger than my JPG?

PNG uses lossless compression, which stores more data than a lossy JPG.

Will the colours change?

No visible change. The pixels are copied as they are.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.

Can I process several images at once?

Yes. Select up to 30 images together, process them in one go, then download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.
