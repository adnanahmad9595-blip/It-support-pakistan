How to use this tool

  1. Select your Excel or CSV file.
  2. Choose the conversion that appears.
  3. Download the converted file.

Tips

  • Open the result in Excel and check that columns, dates and numbers look right.
  • CSV files carry no formatting, so add widths and styles after converting.
  • If characters look wrong, the CSV may use a different encoding.

Questions

Will leading zeros be kept?

Check phone numbers and codes after converting, as spreadsheets can treat them as numbers.

Is my file uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no account, login or daily limit.
